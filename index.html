CallMate

A personal call assistant for Android.

CallMate turns your phone calls into short notes: a summary, key points, a transcript and the tasks that came up, with reminders. It understands Gujarati, Hindi, English and mixes of them.

  • Call notes and tasks stay on your phone.
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive.
  • Optional sync of tasks with your own Google Tasks and Calendar, and of contacts with your Google account.

CallMate is a private app for its owner and invited testers. It is not sold or advertised.
