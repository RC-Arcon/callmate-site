CallMate

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

CallMate is an Android app that turns your phone calls into notes and tasks. This policy explains what data it uses and where that data goes. In short: your data stays on your phone or in your own Google account. CallMate has no server of its own, shows no ads, and does not sell or share your data.

Data on your phone

CallMate stores call recordings, the notes made from them (summary, key points, transcript), tasks, the call-protection log and your settings on your phone. You can delete any call, task or recording in the app, and uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone.

AI processing

To make notes, CallMate sends a call's audio to Google's Gemini API using an API key that you enter in the app. Google processes it under the Gemini API terms. Nothing is sent unless you have added your own key.

Your Google account

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, CallMate asks only for the access each feature needs, and uses it only for that feature, on your behalf:

  • Google Drive: to save and restore your CallMate backups in your own Drive.
  • Google Contacts: to show names for numbers and keep contact details in sync with your account.
  • Google Tasks and Google Calendar: to copy your CallMate tasks and reminders there, if you turn this on.

This data moves only between your phone and your Google account. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else. CallMate's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove CallMate's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

App updates

Test versions are delivered through Firebase App Distribution, which handles your email address and device details under Firebase's privacy terms so it can offer you updates.

Call recording

Recording laws differ by place. You are responsible for following the law where you and the person you are calling are, including telling them when required.

Children

CallMate is not meant for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. For questions, use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen when you sign in to CallMate.
